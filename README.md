## The entry

> Diagnosis: chronic self-diagnosis. You have Googled your symptoms 14 times and arrived at 15 conditions, all of them wrong.

## Why it works

The setup sounds like a chart note; the punchline is the count going the wrong way — fourteen searches somehow produce fifteen conditions, and every one of them is wrong. The laugh lands on the final three words, with nothing left to explain.
